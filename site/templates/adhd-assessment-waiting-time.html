What the council reports

"Once on pathway" suggests this doesn't count time on the waiting list before that, which may be why it is much shorter than the NHS England figures above. The council doesn't say.

What these figures don't tell you

  • They show how long children still waiting have waited so far. They will wait longer before they are seen. The NHS doesn't publish how long a finished wait took.
  • They count referrals for "neurodevelopmental conditions, excluding autism". That includes ADHD but also conditions such as Tourette syndrome, so it may overstate ADHD referrals.
  • They don't include children waiting on community paediatrics lists. Some children are assessed for ADHD there instead (see the NHS community waiting lists).
  • They cover all of North East London, not Waltham Forest alone.
