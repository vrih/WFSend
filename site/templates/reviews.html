Annual reviews

Every EHC plan must be reviewed at least once every 12 months ({{ cite("cfa_2014_s44", "section 44(1)") }}). After the review meeting, the council has 4 weeks to decide whether to keep, amend or end the plan ({{ cite("send_regs_2014", "regulation 20") }}). The DfE's latest annual review figures for each council cover the {{ ar.period }} calendar year. See what should happen at a review.
