How are SEND services in Waltham Forest performing?

Parents have asked Waltham Forest Council for clear performance figures and targets for special educational needs and disability (SEND) services. This site brings together what has already been published by the Department for Education, Ofsted and the council's own committee papers. Every figure links to its source.

What has changed

  • EHC plans are much slower. For four years, Waltham Forest issued about nine in ten new EHC plans within the 20-week legal limit. That fell to {{ fmt(t.series[WF]["2024"]) }} in 2024 and {{ fmt(t.series[WF]["2025"]) }} in {{ t.latest_period }}, {{ rank_text(t) }}. The council's latest figure is {{ fmt(lt.value) }} ({{ cite(lt.source, lt.page) }}). It puts the fall down to delays in educational psychologist advice ({{ cite("hwb_oct2025_report", 4) }}).

They are copied as printed, including the council's own warning that its case management data may be inaccurate ({{ cite(local.data_quality_warning.source, local.data_quality_warning.page) }}).
