The steps and the legal deadlines

  1. Someone asks for an assessment. A parent, a young person or a school can ask the council directly ({{ cite("cfa_2014_s36", "section 36(1)") }}). See how to ask.
  2. The council decides whether to assess: 6 weeks. In {{ d.six_weeks.latest_period }}, Waltham Forest made {{ fmt(d.six_weeks.latest[WF]) }} of these decisions within 6 weeks (England {{ fmt(d.six_weeks.latest["England"]) }}). Most of the delay comes after this step.
  3. Professionals send their advice: 6 weeks from when the council asks them, for example an educational psychologist or a health service ({{ cite("send_regs_2014", "regulation 8") }}). The council says delays in educational psychologist advice are the main reason plans are late ({{ cite("hwb_oct2025_report", 4) }}).
  4. You get a draft plan and at least 15 days to comment and ask for a school ({{ cite("send_regs_2014", "regulation 13(1)") }}).
  5. The final plan: 20 weeks from the request ({{ cite("send_regs_2014", "regulation 13(2)") }}).

Enter the date of your own request in the deadlines calculator to see each date.

What these figures don't tell you

  • They count plans by the year they were issued, so a request made in 2024 that ended in a plan in 2025 counts in 2025.
  • They only include requests that ended with a plan. Families who were refused an assessment, or assessed and refused a plan, aren't counted.
  • They can't tell you how long your own child will wait. That depends on how quickly advice comes in, and on how busy the council is when you apply.

If the deadlines are missed

The deadlines are legal duties, not targets. If one is missed you can complain to the council, and then to the Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman (see complaints and appeals). If the council refuses to assess or refuses to issue a plan, you can appeal to the SEND Tribunal. You must usually speak to a mediation adviser first ({{ cite("cfa_2014_s55", "section 55") }}). For free advice, contact IPSEA or Waltham Forest's SEND information, advice and support service (SENDIASS).
