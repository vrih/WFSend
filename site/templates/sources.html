Rates such as "% within 20 weeks" are recalculated from the published counts. The City of London is left out of London rankings because its numbers are too small to compare.

Similar areas

As well as London and England, many charts compare Waltham Forest with the average of its 10 "statistical neighbours". These are the councils the DfE judges most like Waltham Forest, using census measures such as deprivation, housing, employment and first language. Councils and Ofsted use the same comparison. The 10 similar areas, closest first, are: {% for n in NEIGHBOURS %}{{ n }}{{ ", " if not loop.last and loop.revindex > 2 else (" and " if loop.revindex == 2 else "") }}{% endfor %}.

The similar areas figure is a simple average of the 10 councils' rates, as in the DfE's own local authority interactive tool. It is left blank when fewer than {{ SN_MIN }} of them have a figure. No average is given for counts, because the councils differ in size. Southampton is the only one outside London, so it isn't included in the London rankings.

Population estimates

The chart of requests for an EHC needs assessment shows them per 1,000 children, so areas of different sizes can be compared. It uses the Office for National Statistics (ONS) estimates of the number of children aged 2 to 18 living in each area on 30 June. A script downloads these from Nomis, the ONS's official service for population and labour market data. The latest estimates are for mid-{{ ONS_MANIFEST.latest_year }}. Each year's requests use the estimate for June of that year.

Source: {{ ONS_MANIFEST.publisher }}, {{ ONS_MANIFEST.title }} (published {{ ONS_MANIFEST.published }}, downloaded {{ ONS_MANIFEST.fetched }}). Copy used here: ons_population.csv.

Ombudsman data

Published decisions and headline complaint statistics from the Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman are downloaded by a script, like the DfE data. Last downloaded {{ LGO_MANIFEST.fetched }}. Copies: ombudsman_decisions.csv, ombudsman_annual_stats.csv.

Register of schools

The list of special schools, resourced provision and SEN units on the Places page comes from the DfE's Get Information About Schools, which publishes every school's details each day. A script downloads the file and keeps Waltham Forest's special schools and any school recorded with resourced provision or an SEN unit, including ones that have closed. Last downloaded {{ d.specialist_settings.manifest.fetched }}. Copy: gias_specialist_settings.csv.

Things to bear in mind

  • DfE and council figures measure different things. DfE timeliness counts plans issued in a calendar year. The council's monitoring uses monthly or quarterly snapshots from its own case management system.
  • Data quality. In October 2025 the council said its performance data "may be providing a worst-case scenario as data analytics from our Capita system is inaccurate" ({{ cite(local.data_quality_warning.source, local.data_quality_warning.page) }}). The council did not say whether this also affects the figures it sends to the DfE.
  • Small numbers. Exam results and exclusion rates for pupils with EHC plans are based on small groups and can change a lot from year to year.
  • Provisional data. The latest year of exam results is usually provisional and is revised later.
  • Suppressed figures. DfE hides some small numbers to protect privacy. These show as "–" or "no data".

Gaps

Figures parents have asked for that we have not found published for Waltham Forest:

  • Numeric targets for EHC plan timeliness, annual reviews or plan quality.
  • Regular, dated monitoring of EHC plan timeliness and annual reviews since the September 2025 highlight report. The council's corporate performance dashboard, reported to its Budget, Performance and Coordinating Committee every two months, counts live EHC plans but doesn't track timeliness or reviews. The only later timeliness figure we found is "12% within 20 weeks" in the July 2026 Schools Forum papers, and they don't say what period it covers.
  • The full SEND Reform Plan and its slide pack (Annex A). The July 2026 Schools Forum papers refer to them, but they are not in the published pack.
  • Outcomes of SEND Tribunal appeals for Waltham Forest. National figures are published, but not figures for each council.
  • How many children are waiting for a named school place or for the provision in their plan to be delivered.
