Health services are commissioned by NHS North East London and mostly provided by NELFT. The 2025 inspection asked health leaders to improve waiting times "at pace" for neurodevelopmental assessment, CAMHS, continence and wheelchair services ({{ cite("ofsted_2025", 6) }}).

    NHS waiting lists for children's community services

    NHS England publishes monthly waiting lists for each community health provider. The council's own reports name NELFT (North East London NHS Foundation Trust) as the provider of children's therapies in Waltham Forest ({{ cite("hwb_oct2025_report", 3) }}). NELFT also serves other boroughs, so these figures are not for Waltham Forest alone.

    In Waltham Forest, NELFT's community paediatricians are children's doctors based at Wood Street Health Centre. They run the Social and Communication assessment Clinic (SACC), which assesses children under 5 for autism, and clinics for children with complex physical and learning needs, cerebral palsy, epilepsy and genetic conditions such as Down's syndrome. They also do child protection medicals, health checks for children in care, and medical advice for EHC needs assessments ({{ cite("nelft_wf_community_paeds", "list of services") }}). Children over 5 are assessed for autism by CAMHS instead ({{ cite("lbwf_child_development", "SACC section") }}). So the community paediatrics waiting list includes many kinds of referral. It is not an autism waiting list, and NHS England doesn't say how many children on it are waiting for each clinic.

    Waiting for an autism assessment

    In plain words: how long is the wait for an autism assessment?

    NHS England publishes monthly figures on people referred for a suspected autism assessment. NICE guidance says an autism assessment should start within 3 months (13 weeks) of referral ({{ cite("nice_cg128", "recommendation 1.5.1") }}).

    "NELFT" counts children seen by the trust, which also works outside North East London. "North East London" counts children registered with a GP or living in the NHS North East London area, whichever provider they were referred to. Neither is for Waltham Forest alone. NHS England describes these as experimental statistics.

    Waiting for an ADHD assessment

    In plain words: how long is the wait for an ADHD assessment?

    NHS England publishes monthly management information on people who may be waiting for an ADHD assessment. It counts referrals to NHS mental health services for "neurodevelopmental conditions, excluding autism", which also covers conditions such as dyslexia and Tourette syndrome, so it may overstate ADHD referrals. It doesn't include children waiting on community paediatrics lists, which are shown above. The figures are for NHS North East London as a whole, not Waltham Forest alone, and cover children aged 5 to 17. NHS England rounds them to the nearest 5.

    Waiting for children's mental health services (CAMHS)

    NHS England publishes how long children and young people under 18 wait for a first contact with NHS-funded mental health services, over a rolling 3 months. "NELFT" counts children seen by the trust, which runs CAMHS in Waltham Forest and other boroughs. "North East London" counts children living or registered with a GP in the NHS North East London area. Neither is for Waltham Forest alone.
