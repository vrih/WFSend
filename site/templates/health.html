Health services for children with SEND

Health services are commissioned by NHS North East London and mostly provided by NELFT. The 2025 inspection asked health leaders to improve waiting times "at pace" for neurodevelopmental assessment, CAMHS, continence and wheelchair services ({{ cite("ofsted_2025", 6) }}).

NHS England publishes monthly waiting lists for each community health provider. The council reports that health advice for EHC needs assessments is returned within 6 weeks in "65-90%" of cases ({{ cite("hwb_oct2025_report", 4) }}). The legal deadline is 6 weeks ({{ cite("send_regs_2014", "regulation 8") }}). No exact figure or trend has been published.

In October 2025 the council said waits for occupational therapy "remain approximately one year", partly because therapists' time went into moving to a new community equipment provider. The previous provider went into insolvency on 31 July 2025 ({{ cite("hwb_oct2025_report", "pages 4 and 5") }}).
