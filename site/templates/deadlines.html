Deadlines calculator

Enter a date to see the legal deadlines that follow from it. Nothing you type leaves your browser.

The deadlines are calendar weeks and months. They include school holidays. The law allows a few exceptions, listed under each calculator. This is not legal advice. If a deadline is close or has been missed, get advice from IPSEA or Waltham Forest's SEND information, advice and support service (SENDIASS).

These are the legal deadlines. For how long families in Waltham Forest actually wait, see how long an EHC plan takes, and the waits for an autism assessment or an ADHD assessment.

Exceptions. The 16 and 20 week deadlines don't apply if it is impractical to meet them because the council asked a school or nursery for advice shortly before a closure of 4 weeks or more, because of exceptional personal circumstances, or because the child or family were away from the area for 4 weeks or more ({{ cite("send_regs_2014", "regulation 10(4)") }}). The 6 week deadline has similar exceptions. See how long EHC plans actually take in Waltham Forest.

Moving school. When a child is moving to a new phase of education, for example from primary to secondary school, the plan must be reviewed and amended by 15 February in the year of the move. For a move from secondary school to college or another post-16 setting, the date is 31 March ({{ cite("send_regs_2014", "regulation 18") }}). Every plan must be reviewed at least every 12 months ({{ cite("cfa_2014_s44", "section 44(1)") }}). See how many Waltham Forest reviews are done on time.
