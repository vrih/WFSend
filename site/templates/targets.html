    What the law requires

    DutyDeadlineLatest Waltham Forest figureLaw
    Tell the family whether the council will carry out an EHC needs assessment6 weeks from the request {{ fmt(d.six_weeks.latest[WF]) }} of decisions on time ({{ d.six_weeks.latest_period }}, DfE){{ cite("send_regs_2014", "regulation 5") }}
    Health and social care bodies provide advice for an assessment6 weeks from being asked "65-90%" (October 2025, council){{ cite("send_regs_2014", "regulation 8") }}
    Issue the final EHC plan20 weeks from the request {{ fmt(d.timeliness.latest[WF]) }} on time ({{ d.timeliness.latest_period }}, DfE){{ cite("send_regs_2014", "regulation 13") }}
    Review every EHC planAt least every 12 months {{ fmt(d.ar.completed.latest[WF]) }} of reviews due in {{ d.ar.period }} completed (DfE){{ cite("cfa_2014_s44", "section 44(1)") }}
    Decide the outcome of an annual review4 weeks from the review meeting {{ fmt(d.ar.decision.latest[WF]) }} on time ({{ d.ar.period }}, DfE){{ cite("send_regs_2014", "regulation 20") }}

    The law allows a few exceptions to the 6 and 20 week deadlines, for example when a school is closed for 4 weeks or more. The DfE timeliness figure leaves those cases out.

    The SEND Reform Plan submitted in June 2026 lists "improving statutory timeliness for EHCP processes" as one of five priority goals. The Schools Forum summary gives no number or date for it ({{ cite("sf_jul2026", 178) }}). See what we know about the SEND Reform Plan.

    The SEND Strategy 2025–2028 says the partnership will "develop a local area plan with clear targets that measure if we are realising our vision" ({{ cite("send_strategy_2025", 4) }}). If you know where those targets are published, please get in touch.
