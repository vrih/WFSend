    What happens to appeals across England

    The Ministry of Justice publishes what happens to SEND Tribunal appeals, but only for England as a whole. It does not publish outcomes for each council, so these figures are not about Waltham Forest.

    What tribunal cases cost the council

    The council's SEND Sufficiency Plan says its legal costs for tribunal cases were £48,860 in 2024, up from £26,197 in 2023, not counting barristers' fees ({{ cite("sufficiency_plan_2025", 27) }}). But in June 2026, in answer to a Freedom of Information request, the council said "Tribunal expenditure is not recorded" ({{ cite("foi_send_costs_jun2026", 1) }}). It also said that finding out how many independent school placements followed a tribunal order, or were conceded during an appeal, would cost more than the FOI cost limit allows ({{ cite("foi_send_costs_jun2026", 2) }}). The council's published payments don't help either: legal fees are coded to its legal service, not to SEND (see payments to suppliers).

    Complaints to the Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman

    {% set o = d.ombudsman %}

    Families who are unhappy with how the council handled a complaint can go to the Ombudsman. The Ombudsman can look at delays and failures to deliver what a plan says. It usually won't look at decisions that can be appealed to the SEND Tribunal.
