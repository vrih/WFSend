High needs funding and the schools budget deficit

Support for children with EHC plans is mostly paid for from the High Needs Block of the Dedicated Schools Grant (DSG), a ring-fenced government grant. Spending above the grant builds up as a DSG deficit. A government "statutory override" currently keeps that deficit off the council's main budget, until March 2028 ({{ cite("sf_oct2026", 31) }}).

The SEND Reform Plan

Waltham Forest was not in either of the DfE's intervention programmes for councils with deficits, Safety Valve and Delivering Better Value ({{ cite("budget_feb2026", 33) }}). In June 2026, like every council, it had to send the DfE a SEND Reform Plan setting out how it will deliver the national SEND reforms within its budget ({{ cite("sf_jul2026", 177) }}). The DfE accepted the plan on 2 October 2026, with an indicative High Needs Stability Grant of £12.889m. The council now reports to the DfE on the plan every quarter ({{ cite("sf_oct2026", "pages 30 and 31") }}). The plan's targets are on the targets page.

We have not found the full plan published. In July the council said a detailed breakdown of the plan's spending, and of its "Experts at Hand" support for schools, would come to the Schools Forum in October 2026 ({{ cite("sf_jul2026", 177) }}). It was not on the October agenda ({{ cite("sf_oct2026", 2) }}).

Payments to suppliers

Each month the council publishes every payment of £500 or more it makes to suppliers. These are the payments it coded to its SEND services. They show who the money goes to. They don't cover everything the council spends on SEND: payments under £500 and staff pay aren't listed.

Why only these years: the reports from {{ pay.first_coded_label }} onwards use the council's current finance codes, and earlier reports can't be matched to these services. From April 2026 the reports list far fewer payments for every service, not just SEND: {{ "{:,.0f}".format(pay.after.payments) }} a month on average, against {{ "{:,.0f}".format(pay.before.payments) }} in {{ ly }}. We have left 2026/27 out until the council explains the change.
