High needs funding and the schools budget deficit

Support for children with EHC plans is mostly paid for from the High Needs Block of the Dedicated Schools Grant (DSG), a ring-fenced government grant. Spending above the grant builds up as a DSG deficit. A government "statutory override" currently keeps that deficit off the council's main budget, until March 2028 ({{ cite("schools_budget_mar2026", 7) }}).

Why this matters for families. The council's draft deficit management plan relies on "reducing the number of requests to assess children whose needs can be met outside the statutory framework" ({{ cite("budget_feb2026", 34) }}). Families have a legal right to request an assessment. Any change in how requests are handled should show up in the request and refusal figures.
