Source: NHS England, Autism waiting time statistics (experimental statistics). Children aged 0 to 17.

NICE guidance says an autism assessment should start within 3 months (13 weeks) of referral ({{ cite("nice_cg128", "recommendation 1.5.1") }}).

"NELFT" is North East London NHS Foundation Trust, the NHS trust that runs children's community and mental health services in Waltham Forest. It also works in other boroughs. "North East London" counts children living in or registered with a GP in the NHS North East London area, whoever they were referred to. Neither is for Waltham Forest alone.

What these figures don't tell you

  • The wait is to the first assessment appointment. Getting a diagnosis can take longer.
  • The median is the middle wait. Half of children waited longer.
  • In April 2026 the number of children NELFT reports as waiting fell by about 90% in a single month, with no explanation from NHS England. A change in how NELFT records data is more likely than a real fall. Until it is explained, this page uses figures up to {{ pm }}. The later months are on the health page.
