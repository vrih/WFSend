    Children in need and children in care

    Children known to children's social care are much more likely than other pupils to have special educational needs. The DfE matches social care records with school records to show how many have an EHC plan or SEN support. It splits them into three groups, counted at 31 March each year:

    • children in care: looked after by the council for at least 12 months
    • children on a child protection plan, who are not in care
    • other children in need: children assessed as needing help from children's social care, including disabled children, who are not on a child protection plan or in care

    Children are counted in the council responsible for them, wherever they go to school. Across England, {{ fmt(cla.latest["England"]) }} of children in care had an EHC plan in {{ cla.latest_period }}, compared with {{ fmt(d.prevalence.ehcp.series["England"][all_jan]) }} of all pupils in {{ all_jan|month }}.
